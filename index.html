CMSC 206 · Database Management Systems

Study Guides

The course follows one question all the way down: how does a pile of facts become data you can trust, share, and query? Each topic adds another guarantee to the system you’ll eventually design yourself.

Structure how data is organised
Integrity what stays true
Access who gets what

Phase 1 · Foundations

01 Fundamental DBMS Concepts Databases and database management systems, the drawbacks of plain files, ACID and BASE, levels of abstraction, and types of DBMS. →

Phase 2 · Designing the Schema

2.1 Data Models · The ER Model Entities, attributes, and relationships; keys, cardinality, and participation; weak entities, and the notation used to draw an ER diagram. → 2.2 The Relational Model Relations, tuples, attributes, domains and degree, the properties of a valid table, and the five cases for turning an ER diagram into a working schema. → 03 Normalization Redundancy and anomalies, functional dependencies, 1NF through BCNF, and lossless decomposition with Heath’s theorem. →

Phase 3 · Querying the Schema

04 Relational Algebra Select, project, and rename; the set operations and type compatibility; cross product, the three kinds of join, division, and the outer operations. →
05 SQL Relational algebra in the language you will actually type. Soon
