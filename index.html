How to use these pages

A few things worth knowing before you start.

Work, don’t skim

The diagrams, trees, and card decks are tap-to-open on purpose. Predict the answer before you open one — that’s where the learning happens.

Progress isn’t saved

Reloading a page starts the Check Your Understanding over. Finish a topic in one sitting, or note the items you missed before you leave.

These supplement the lectures

Every page is adapted from the course slide deck for its topic. Read them alongside the original material, not instead of it.
